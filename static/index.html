How it works

From a match to a written agreement

Most tools stop at introductions. PartnerPortal takes you from finding an organization whose needs line up with yours to a summary both sides have agreed to.

  1. Step 1

    Describe your organization

    Pick what you need and what you can offer from 33 shared categories.

  2. Step 2

    See your matches

    Every organization ranked by how well the two profiles fit, in both directions.

  3. Step 3

    Propose terms

    Start from a proposal pre-filled with the overlap that produced the match.

  4. Step 4

    Confirm and share

    Both sides sign off, and you get a summary page to show a board or a funder.
