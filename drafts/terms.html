You give us permission to store it and show it as the site works: your profile to other organizations and, in the public parts, to anyone; your messages to the organization you sent them to; an agreement's terms to the other party and, once accepted, to anyone holding the agreement's link. We may take a profile out of the directory
If a profile breaks the rules above, or a name is not one we can show, we may remove it from the directory and from match results. You are told the reason by email, you can still sign in, and any partnership you have already agreed is unaffected. We may close an account outright for repeated or serious misuse.

Matches and agreements

A match is a ranking, not a recommendation
Matching compares what two profiles say they need and offer. We do not verify what any organization says about itself, and a high score is not a statement by us that an organization is who it says it is or will do what it offers. Look before you agree, as you would anywhere.
An agreement summary is a record, not a contract
When both organizations accept a proposal, the site records the terms each side committed to and makes a summary page that either side can share. That page says what was agreed on PartnerPortal. It is not a contract drafted by us, we are not a party to it, and whether it is enforceable between the two organizations is a matter for them and for the law that applies to them. If you need a binding agreement, make one.
Delivery is between the two of you
PartnerPortal does not perform, supervise or guarantee any part of a partnership. Whether an organization provides what it committed to is its responsibility, and any dispute about it is between the organizations involved. Each side may record whether the other delivered; that record is visible only to the two of you and is not a finding by us.
